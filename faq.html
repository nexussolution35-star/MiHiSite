Frequently asked questions

The things Lowveld homeowners most often ask us before starting a project. Still not sure? We're happy to talk it through.

What materials do you work with?

We build in solid wood, semi-solid timber, Melawood (melamine) and hand-sprayed Duco paint finishes, plus granite and quartz for countertops. In your design consultation we help you choose the right material for each part of the project, balancing look, durability and budget.

How much does a bespoke kitchen or cupboard cost?

Every project is made to measure, so pricing depends on size, materials and finishes. We provide a clear, itemised quote after a free design consultation so you know exactly what you're paying for — with no surprises.

How long does a project take?

Most kitchens and cupboard projects take a few weeks from approved design to installation, depending on the size of the job and our current workshop schedule. We confirm realistic timelines with you before we start.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Rocky Drift, White River, and design and install throughout the Mpumalanga Lowveld — including Mbombela (Nelspruit), Hazyview, Sabie, Barberton and Malelane.

Do you handle installation yourselves?

Yes. One Mi-Hi team designs, manufactures and installs your joinery. The people who build your kitchen are the people who fit it, so nothing is lost between the workshop and your home.

Is there a deposit, and how does payment work?

Bespoke work is typically confirmed with a deposit once you approve the design and quote, with the balance due on completion. We'll set out the exact payment terms in writing with your quote.

Can I visit a showroom?

Absolutely. Visit our Rocky Drift showroom at 140A Solomon St, White River — open Monday to Friday 8:00–16:30 and Saturday 8:00–13:00. Pop in to see finishes in person or book a consultation.
